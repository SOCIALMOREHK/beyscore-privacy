Privacy Policy — BEYSCORE X

Last updated: 23 June 2026

BEYSCORE X (“the App”) is a Beyblade X battle scorer. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We have no servers and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your teams, beyblade setups, player name, and battle history are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the App removes this data.

Camera

Used solely to scan a QR code to join a nearby battle session. No images are stored or transmitted.

Local Network

Used solely to connect with other devices on the same Wi-Fi for multiplayer battles. No data leaves your local network.

Children

The App does not collect any data and is safe for all ages.
