Privacy Policy — BEYSCORE X

Last updated: 12 July 2026

BEYSCORE X (“the App”) is a Beyblade X battle scorer. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. We have no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking. We do not sell, rent, or store your personal data on any server.

Data stored on your device

Your teams, beyblade setups, player name, and battle history are stored on your device. Deleting the App removes this data. The only time information leaves your device is the real-time multiplayer relay described below.

Multiplayer & online play

For multiplayer, devices connect either directly over your local Wi-Fi, or — when they are on different networks — through a third-party real-time relay service (Supabase). During an online session, only the information needed to run the match (your chosen display name and gameplay data such as scores and team selections) is sent in real time to the other players in your session through this relay. This data is relayed live to keep the match in sync; it is not stored on our servers or by the relay, and we keep no accounts and no server-side records.

Camera

Used solely to scan a QR code to join a battle session. No images are stored or transmitted.

Children

The App has no accounts and no tracking, and is safe for all ages.
